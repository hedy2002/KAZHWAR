A little care goes a long way

A happier plant starts here.

Simple guidance for healthy roots, leaves, and blooms.

💧

Watering

Check the top 2–3 cm of soil. Water when dry and let excess drain away.

☀

Sunlight

Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect light; protect leaves from harsh midday sun.

🌱

Everyday care

Use a pot with drainage, turn plants occasionally, and follow care notes for each variety.
